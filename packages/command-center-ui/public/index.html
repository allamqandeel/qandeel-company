Reading the company

  • The gold trunk runs from the Founder to the CEO; the gold bus leads from the CEO into the five departments.
  • A column is a department: its Director first, then Managers and Leads, then Specialists, on one lane in the department's colour.
  • A dashed card is a vacant seat; “Acting cover” marks a seat held on behalf of someone.
  • The dot on a person is their status: green well, blue running now (with a circling ring), amber awaiting a decision, red blocked.
  • Goals along the bottom are the strategic direction. Each goal has a gold rail: the departments whose work serves it drop into the rail, and the bundle enters the goal. A gold diamond on a card means that person serves a goal.
  • Beside the Founder: what needs you (approvals, briefs, decisions). Rest on one to see where it lives in the company. Nothing else is decorative.
