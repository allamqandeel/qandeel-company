Reading the universe

  • The Founder is the centre. The CEO is on the nearest orbit; every orbit further out is one rank lower.
  • A sector is a department: its name sits on the coloured rim, and every person in it carries the same hue.
  • Hollow = waiting (review or approval)
  • Broken ring = blocked
  • Dashed = vacant seat
  • Double ring = acting cover
  • Circling arc = work running now
  • A beacon outside the last orbit is a goal, tethered to the departments serving it.
  • Inside the CEO orbit = needs you
  • Lit lines are live relations only (delegation, review, approval, escalation). Nothing is decorative.
