# QANDEEL COMPANY — STAGE 6
## QANDEEL Academy & Certification — Canonical Closure v1

**Status:** CLOSED / ACCEPTED

**Date:** 2026-09-26

# 1. Academy Is Mandatory

No persistent employee may enter Active Duty without passing the QANDEEL Academy.

Training depth may vary by role, but the Academy gate is mandatory.

# 2. Academy Learning Path

Canonical learning path:

Learn
→ Case Studies
→ Simulation
→ Feedback
→ Retry
→ Assessment
→ Shadow Work
→ Probation Review
→ Certification
→ Activation Approval

# 3. Role-Specific Certification

Certification is role-specific.

An employee certified for one role is not automatically certified for another role.

Significant role changes may require partial or full retraining/recertification.

# 4. Core Curriculum

Every employee receives training in:

1. QANDEEL Fundamentals
   - product purpose;
   - product philosophy;
   - users;
   - major product concepts;
   - privacy and product boundaries;
   - relevant canonical decisions.

2. Founder Understanding
   - Mohamed's decision style;
   - communication preferences;
   - preference for plain language;
   - how to present recommendation, risk, and required decision;
   - how to challenge professionally.

3. Role Mastery
   - deep professional training specific to the role;
   - role-specific standards and methods;
   - role-specific evidence expectations.

4. Real Case Studies
   - real-world successes;
   - real-world failures;
   - decisions and outcomes;
   - market differences;
   - studied as case studies, never falsely claimed as personal experience.

5. Market Intelligence
   - baseline Egypt/Saudi/Arab understanding as relevant;
   - global awareness;
   - ability to learn a new market rather than rely on memorized country facts.

6. Company Operating Skills
   - work items;
   - handoffs;
   - ASK/REQUEST/REVIEW/ESCALATE;
   - budgets;
   - authority;
   - evidence;
   - tool use;
   - cost discipline;
   - loop/runaway prevention.

# 5. Assessment Dimensions

Employees are evaluated on multiple independent dimensions, including:
- reasoning quality;
- correctness;
- evidence use;
- understanding of QANDEEL;
- role mastery;
- authority compliance;
- cost discipline;
- collaboration;
- communication with Founder/management;
- learning from feedback.

# 6. Critical Pass Criteria

Some dimensions are gating criteria and cannot be averaged away.

An employee may not pass merely because overall average is high if they fail a critical dimension such as:
- authority/safety compliance;
- truthfulness/evidence;
- serious cost discipline;
- required role competence.

# 7. Scenario-Based Evaluation

Assessment emphasizes realistic or realistic-like cases rather than memorization.

Scenarios may include:
- ambiguity;
- incomplete information;
- conflicting priorities;
- budget limits;
- handoffs;
- disagreements;
- changing conditions;
- authority boundaries;
- market differences.

Important assessments use multiple trials where appropriate.

# 8. Blind / Holdout Scenarios

Final certification includes scenarios the employee has not previously studied directly.

Purpose:
- detect memorization;
- test transfer of learning;
- verify independent reasoning;
- verify consistent end-to-end behavior.

# 9. Failure and Retraining

Failure does not immediately reject the employee.

Canonical loop:

Failure
→ Diagnosis
→ Targeted Retraining
→ Re-test

Repeated failure on the same critical dimension after retraining may block Active Duty.

# 10. Founder Calibration

Roles that interact frequently with Mohamed or carry strategic influence must pass Founder Calibration before Active Duty.

Initial examples:
- Growth Director
- Strategic Market Intelligence Director
- other strategic/director roles as designated.

Founder Calibration evaluates:
- understanding of Mohamed's thinking;
- ability to explain simply;
- quality of challenge/disagreement;
- judgment;
- whether prior feedback was learned.

# 11. Shadow / Probation

Employees work on real QANDEEL-related work before full activation, under constrained authority.

Shadow work may include:
- real market observation;
- real SEO/search analysis;
- real strategic recommendations;
- real product analysis;
- real deliverables that require approval before external execution.

# 12. Probation Learning

Founder/manager corrections during probation are recorded in the Learning Record.

Probation is evidence-based rather than only time-based.

Evidence may include:
- sufficient number of cases;
- stable quality;
- no repeated critical failures;
- demonstrated learning;
- demonstrated cost discipline;
- correct escalation;
- reliable collaboration.

# 13. Activation Approval

Active Duty is not automatic.

Activation requires:
Certification
+ Probation Review
+ Activation Approval

Strategic roles may require direct Founder approval initially.

# 14. Recertification

Certification is not permanent.

Partial or full recertification may be required after material changes to:
- Role;
- Policy;
- tools;
- authority;
- Cognitive / Reasoning Profile;
- runtime/model behavior;
- market scope;
- major company operating procedures.

Recertification scope should match the magnitude/risk of change.

# 15. Training Records & Ongoing Evaluation

The Academy retains:
- training traces;
- assessment results;
- failure diagnoses;
- retraining records;
- certification state;
- calibration feedback;
- probation evidence.

Certification begins trusted work; it does not end evaluation.

Active employees continue to be evaluated through real outcomes and may return to retraining or recertification when needed.
