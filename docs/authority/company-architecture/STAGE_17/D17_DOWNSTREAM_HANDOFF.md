## Carry Forward to Stage 18

Stage 18 must validate the Strong v1 system through a controlled pilot. It should prove, rather than redesign, the Stage 0–17 contracts.

Pilot must validate at minimum:
- persistent employee identity independent of model/provider/runtime;
- model routing and bounded reasoning/cost behavior;
- governed tool use, permissions, secrets, and egress;
- durable work execution, crash recovery, backup/restore paths;
- Founder conversational management and approval flow;
- role-specific performance profiles;
- Review Pool calibration and uncertainty handling;
- causal failure attribution;
- regression/Gold Case learning loop;
- cost-per-qualified-outcome accounting;
- Founder daily/weekly/monthly reporting usefulness;
- skill-gap/re-certification signals;
- no silent expensive fallback, uncontrolled agent loops, unbounded delegation, or unauthorized high-risk actions.

## Mandatory Pre-Implementation Dependency
Environment Readiness Gate remains mandatory before any real implementation or pilot execution. The controlled pilot must not begin until the environment gate passes and its manifest is produced.

## Scope Discipline
Stage 18 should not reopen closed Product/Architecture decisions unless implementation evidence reveals a real contradiction or safety/correctness failure.
