# QANDEEL COMPANY — STAGE 4
## Employee Identity & Lifecycle — Canonical Closure v1

**Status:** CLOSED / ACCEPTED  
**Gate:** B4 — PASSED  
**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Weight:** 5% of Strong v1

# 1. Persistent Employee Identity

A persistent Employee is a durable company entity independent from:
- LLM provider;
- model;
- session;
- run;
- task.

Employee identity persists through model/session/runtime changes.

# 2. Employee Identity Package

Each persistent employee will have:
- stable Employee ID;
- Egyptian two-part human-style name;
- visual portrait/avatar;
- current Role and Position;
- Department and Manager;
- professional CV/profile;
- distinct personality and communication style;
- professional strengths and specialties;
- market/domain expertise;
- Cognitive / Reasoning Profile;
- QANDEEL employment record;
- capability growth record;
- training and certification history;
- professional portfolio;
- current status and work state.

Names belong to employees, not roles. A Role change does not create a new person.

# 3. Professional Background vs Real QANDEEL History

Professional Profile may contain a carefully designed pre-QANDEEL professional background appropriate to the employee's elite role capability.

It must remain distinct from:
- real QANDEEL employment history;
- real QANDEEL achievements;
- actual training/certification;
- actual projects completed inside QANDEEL.

No fictional pre-QANDEEL claim may be represented as a real QANDEEL achievement.

# 4. Real Case Study Learning

Employees may study real external projects, launches, campaigns, failures, expansions, and market cases relevant to their roles.

These are recorded as:
**Studied Case Studies**

They are not represented as projects the employee personally worked on.

Each role may have a Case Study Curriculum.

# 5. Personality

Each employee has a distinct, stable personality and working style.

Examples may include:
- practical;
- playful;
- analytical;
- bold;
- calm;
- direct;
- exploratory.

Personality can mature gradually through experience, but:
- cannot override truth, policy, or professionalism;
- should not collapse into identical personalities across employees;
- may not change radically from a single run/session/model;
- material personality changes are controlled and versioned.

# 6. Cognitive / Reasoning Profile

Each Employee has a Cognitive / Reasoning Profile independent from any specific model.

It defines the intended default cognitive style for the role, including concepts such as:
- reasoning depth;
- speed preference;
- cost discipline;
- when to escalate to deeper analysis;
- when lightweight execution is sufficient.

Task requirements may temporarily adjust reasoning intensity within policy and budget.

The system should not use maximum reasoning for every employee or every task.

Detailed runtime mapping to Qwen, DeepSeek, or future providers is deferred to Stage 13.

# 7. Identity Kernel & Boot

Every Employee has an Identity Kernel including at minimum:
- identity;
- current role;
- manager;
- personality;
- professional style;
- authority references;
- communication style;
- current lifecycle state.

On every new session/run, the company system rebuilds employee context from persistent state.

The system does not rely on hidden model/session memory for employee continuity.

Boot context should include only relevant:
- identity;
- company state;
- current assignments;
- open dependencies/messages;
- relevant memory;
- relevant canonical truth.

It should not load the employee's entire history by default.

# 8. Employee Lifecycle

Canonical lifecycle supports:
Candidate
→ Training
→ Shadow / Probation
→ Active

Additional states:
- Paused
- On Leave
- Retraining
- Suspended
- Retired / Former Employee

Employees do not enter Active duty before required training/certification.

# 9. Role Change & Promotion

- Promotion preserves the same Employee ID and identity.
- Role changes are historically recorded.
- The prior role is closed with a transition date.
- The new role starts with a clear effective date.
- Significant role changes may require training/recertification.
- Career history remains continuous.

# 10. Professional Growth

Employees should improve over time through:
- training;
- real work;
- validated lessons;
- reviews;
- simulations;
- studied case studies;
- actual QANDEEL outcomes.

Capability Growth is recorded separately from the original professional background.

Validated improvement may affect:
- capabilities;
- autonomy eligibility;
- training status;
- responsibilities;
- future role eligibility.

# 11. Professional Portfolio

Each employee has a QANDEEL Professional Portfolio containing:
- significant real QANDEEL work;
- studied case studies;
- simulation results;
- certifications;
- validated lessons;
- notable growth;
- performance evidence.

The portfolio must distinguish real work from studied/simulated work.

# 12. Concurrent Runs

One Employee may have multiple authorized runs/tasks, but remains one Employee.

Concurrent runs:
- do not create duplicate identities;
- do not create separate permanent memories by default;
- must reconcile outputs through company state/work rules;
- remain subject to authority, budget, and concurrency controls.

# 13. Acting Coverage

If an Employee is temporarily unavailable:
- a different Employee or Temporary Task Agent may receive Acting Coverage;
- only the necessary work/context/authority is delegated;
- the substitute does not become the original employee;
- original identity, memory, portrait, personality, and career history remain distinct;
- authority automatically ends according to the coverage scope.

# 14. Retire / Former Employee

Permanent departure is represented as Retired / Former Employee rather than deletion.

The employee:
- loses active authority/access;
- remains in company history;
- preserves valid employment records and historical attribution;
- can be referenced by past decisions/work.

Privacy/legal deletion policies remain separate.
