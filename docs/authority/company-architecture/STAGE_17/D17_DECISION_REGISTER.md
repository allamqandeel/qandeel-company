# D17 Decision Register

## D17-A — Performance Measurement Foundation
Status: ACCEPTED

- Completed != Reviewed != Outcome Verified.
- Measure Company → Department → Employee → Work Item.
- Role-specific scorecards.
- Quality/outcomes over raw activity.
- Offline evals + production outcomes.
- Provider-independent evaluation ownership.
- No simplistic universal performance score.

## Founder Decision 1 — Performance Profile
Status: ACCEPTED
No single global employee score. Multi-dimensional, role-specific profile with hard gates where necessary.

## Founder Decision 2 — Causal Attribution
Status: ACCEPTED
Separate employee judgment from model, tool, context, workflow, provider, requirement, and external dependency causes.

## Founder Decision 3 — Consequence Authority
Status: ACCEPTED
Automatic bounded quality controls allowed; autonomous promotion/demotion/termination prohibited.

## Founder Decision 4 — Learning Boundary
Status: ACCEPTED
Continuous experiential learning allowed; consequential skill/workflow changes require evidence/eval; identity/authority/governance cannot self-modify.

## Founder Decision 5 — Failure-to-Learning Pipeline
Status: ACCEPTED
Real failures become permanent regression/Gold Cases only after cause validation, review, and calibration. Holdouts remain hidden.

## Founder Decision 6 — Founder Reporting Cadence
Status: ACCEPTED
Daily brief, weekly operating review, monthly people/capability review, plus on-demand natural-language queries.

## Derived Technical Decisions
Status: ACCEPTED BY CONSOLIDATION

- QANDEEL-owned Eval Registry.
- Provider-independent scorecards and reporting semantics.
- Minimum-sample/uncertainty rules.
- Review Pool calibration and drift monitoring.
- Trace/process evaluation where relevant.
- Cost-per-qualified-outcome economics.
- Skill-gap detection and re-certification signals.
- Regression suite lifecycle.
- Company/department/employee/work-item trend reporting.
- Raw activity metrics are observability only, not performance truth.
